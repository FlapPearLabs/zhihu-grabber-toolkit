# Pilot lessons and direction corrections

The benchmark process itself exposed repeated contract/model failures: evaluation leakage, metric-definition mistakes, unresolved denominators, provenance mistakes, global source-label leakage, provenance/stance conflation, expertise evidence gating, sanitizer loss, off-topic Gold contamination, unsupported historical authority, and first-D2 namespace reconciliation.

The corrected D2 result should be read narrowly:

- The harness can distinguish strategies.
- Synthetic dominance cases show why coverage constraints can matter.
- Real adjudicated cases do **not** establish B2 > B1.
- B2 is materially more expensive in relative compute operations.
- B1/B2 remain lexical n-gram proxies, not real dense semantic baselines.
- Question-level coverage remains unresolved; lanes do not guarantee minority-question preservation.

Therefore the experiment is evidence **against prematurely freezing the current selector architecture**, not evidence that Research Coverage as a product goal is invalid.
